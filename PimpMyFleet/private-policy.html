Pimp My Fleet - Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

Data Collection

Pimp My Fleet does not collect, store, process, or share any personal data.

The app does not require users to create an account and does not transmit any data to the developer or to third parties.

Internet and Server Communication

Pimp My Fleet does not communicate with any external server, backend, or online service.

The app operates entirely on the user's device.

Third-Party Services

Pimp My Fleet does not use third-party services for analytics, advertising, tracking, crash reporting, or personal data collection.

Permissions and Location

Pimp My Fleet does not collect or track the user's location and does not use device information for tracking or data collection.

Data Storage

Pimp My Fleet does not store personal data or transmit user data to external services.

Children's Privacy

Pimp My Fleet does not collect personal information from users, including children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the functionality of Pimp My Fleet changes. Any updated version will be published at this privacy policy URL.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or Pimp My Fleet, you can contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Google Play Store listing.
